# Kinetic Chain Performance Program v2

**Athlete context:** Recreational soccer player, two matches per week (Sunday, Wednesday). Program goal: lower-body strength progression, mobility and range of motion, balance, and injury prevention around match load. Return-to-running protocol and formal retest gates have been retired. Progression is governed by two in-session rules (see Advancement Rules).

**Equipment:** Adjustable dumbbells 5-50 lbs, bench, pull-up bar, resistance bands, foam roller, massage gun, yoga mat, wall, stairs.

---

## 1. Weekly Template

| Day | Session | Duration | Notes |
|-----|---------|----------|-------|
| Sunday | Match + post-match mobility | Match ~90 min + 10 min | Mobility block earlier in day as pre-match warm-up |
| Monday | Recovery + daily mobility block | 15-18 min | Foam roll, massage gun optional add-ons |
| Tuesday | Strength (moderate) + daily mobility block | ~45 min + block | ~85% of Friday loads. Precedes Wednesday fixture |
| Wednesday | Match + post-match mobility | Match ~40 min + 10 min | Mobility block earlier in day as pre-match warm-up |
| Thursday | Recovery + daily mobility block | 15-18 min | Foam roll, massage gun optional add-ons |
| Friday | Strength (primary) + daily mobility block | ~45 min + block | Full loads. 48 hr buffer from both matches |
| Saturday | Easy jog + daily mobility block | 25 min jog (cap 30) | Zone 1-2, conversational pace, flat route. Block after jog |

**Saturday jog rules:**
- Strictly conversational pace. If full sentences are hard, slow down.
- Hard cap at 30 minutes. Aerobic maintenance only, matches provide intensity.
- If legs feel heavy Sunday morning or match performance dips, trim to 20 min or move the jog to Monday and make Saturday full rest.

## 2. Strength Sessions

Two sessions per week. Friday runs full prescribed loads. Tuesday runs the same exercises at approximately 85% of Friday loads, rounded to available dumbbell increments.

**Rest:** 90 sec between sets, 2 min between blocks.

### Block A: Single-Leg Strength (Phase 3)

| Exercise | Sets x Reps | Tempo | Cues | Equipment |
|----------|-------------|-------|------|-----------|
| Bulgarian split squat | 3 x 8/side | 3 sec down, 1 sec pause, 2 sec up | Rear foot on bench. Front shin near vertical. Knee tracks over second toe | Bench, dumbbells |
| Single-leg RDL | 3 x 8/side | 3 sec down, 1 sec pause, 2 sec up | Dumbbell in hand opposite stance leg. Hinge at hip, free leg extends behind | Dumbbell |
| Eccentric step-down | 3 x 8/side | 4 sec lower, tap heel, 2 sec up | Stand on bench. Knee tracks over second toe, no medial collapse. Film from front periodically | Bench, dumbbell when loaded |

### Block B: Core Anti-Extension

| Exercise | Sets x Reps | Tempo | Cues | Equipment |
|----------|-------------|-------|------|-----------|
| Dead bugs (banded from Week 3) | 3 x 8/side | 2 sec extend, 2 sec return | Low back flat to floor throughout | Yoga mat, band |
| Plank with PPT | 3 x 40 sec (45 sec from Week 3) | Isometric | Squeeze glutes, tuck tailbone, hold posterior pelvic tilt | Yoga mat |
| DB pullover | 3 x 10 | 3 sec lower, 1 sec pause, 2 sec return | Stop the moment the low back wants to arch | Bench, dumbbell |

### 6-Week Load Progression (Friday loads; Tuesday ~85%)

| Exercise | Weeks 1-2 | Weeks 3-4 | Weeks 5-6 |
|----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Bulgarian split squat | 10 lbs/hand (Tue: BW or 5) | 15 lbs/hand (Tue: 10) | 20-25 lbs/hand (Tue: 15) |
| Single-leg RDL | 15 lbs (Tue: 10) | 20 lbs (Tue: 15) | 25 lbs (Tue: 20) |
| Eccentric step-down | Bodyweight (Tue: BW) | 10 lbs at chest (Tue: BW) | 15 lbs at chest (Tue: 10) |
| Dead bugs | Unbanded | Banded | Banded |
| Plank with PPT | 3 x 40 sec | 3 x 45 sec | 3 x 45 sec |
| DB pullover | 15 lbs | 20 lbs | 20 lbs |

## 3. Daily Mobility Block (revised, bilateral ankle)

Flow-based. No rest between exercises within a section. Rest 60 sec between sections only. Runs every day of the week, including match days (doubles as pre-match warm-up). Total ~15-18 min.

### Section 1: Ankle Flow (bilateral daily, ~6-7 min)

| Step | Exercise | Prescription | Cues |
|------|----------|--------------|------|
| A1 | Gastroc wall stretch | 2 x 45 sec/side | Knee straight, heel pressed into floor |
| A2 | Soleus wall stretch | 2 x 45 sec/side | Bend back knee ~20 degrees, no position change from A1 |
| A3 | Banded talocrural mobilization | 2 x 10/side | Band at ankle crease pulling posteriorly, heel stays down |
| A4 | Eccentric calf raises | 2 x 15/side | Rise on both feet 2 sec, lower on one leg 3 sec, stair edge for range |

### Section 1b: Right MTP Mobilization (right foot only, daily, ~3 min)

| Step | Exercise | Prescription | Cues |
|------|----------|--------------|------|
| M1 | Passive MTP mobilization | 2 x 10-15 reps, 3-4 sec hold at end range | Stabilize metatarsal head with opposite hand, push toe straight upward, not laterally |
| M2 | Toe extension stretch | 3 x 30 sec | Sustained hold at end range |

Rest 60 sec, then Section 2.

### Section 2: Hip Rotation Flow (~4 min, all days)

| Step | Exercise | Prescription | Cues |
|------|----------|--------------|------|
| H1 | 90/90 ER stretch, left | 3 x 30 sec | Tall spine, hinge over front leg |
| H2 | 90/90 IR stretch, left | 3 x 30 sec | Weight shift toward back leg, no repositioning |
| H3 | 90/90 ER stretch, right | 3 x 30 sec | Swap legs, same cues |
| H4 | 90/90 IR stretch, right | 3 x 30 sec | Weight shift toward back leg |
| H5 | Hip CARs, both sides | 3 circles/direction/side, 10-15 sec per circle | Largest possible arc, no pelvic compensation |

Rest 60 sec, then Section 3.

### Section 3: Hip Flexor Flow (~4 min, all days)

| Step | Exercise | Prescription | Cues |
|------|----------|--------------|------|
| P1 | Half-kneeling hip flexor stretch with PPT | 2 x 45 sec/side | Squeeze rear glute, tuck tailbone, lean forward. Feel at front of rear hip, not low back |
| P2 | Foam roll quads/RF | 2 min/side | Hip to just above knee, slow passes, extra time on tender spots |

## 4. Post-Match Mobility (Sunday and Wednesday, ~10 min)

- Half-kneeling hip flexor stretch with PPT: 2 x 30 sec/side
- 90/90 ER and IR: 1 x 30 sec each position, each side
- Gastroc and soleus wall stretch: 1 x 45 sec each, both sides
- Massage gun on psoas region: 90 sec/side (optional, as tolerated)

## 5. Sunday/Recovery Add-Ons (optional, as time allows)

- Supine hip IR stretch with band: 2 x 30 sec/side
- Foam roll adductors and lateral hip: 90 sec/area
- Massage gun general lower body

## 6. Advancement Rules

Only two rules govern progression. Both must pass to advance loads on schedule.

1. **Form rule:** Front knee tracks over the second toe on Bulgarian split squats and step-downs with no medial collapse. If the knee caves, hold the current load until it tracks clean.
2. **Symptom rule:** No next-morning increase in knee symptoms after any session or match. If symptoms increase, repeat the current week's loads instead of advancing.

**Behavioral cue (ongoing, both sides):** Deliberate big-toe push-off during walking and running. Right side requires conscious cueing due to MTP stiffness. Metatarsal pad soreness under appropriate load is expected adaptation, not a red flag.

---

## 7. Monitoring

- **Monthly WBLT spot check:** Feet straight on tile grout lines, knee to wall, heel flat, measure to 0.25 inch. If left falls more than 0.5 inch behind right, restore daily left-side ankle bias (3 sets left daily, right 3x/week).
- **Gear:** Compression sleeve on right knee optional for matches. Increasing reliance on it signals a load tolerance issue worth addressing.
- **Red flags requiring stopping and evaluation:** joint effusion or swelling, night pain, locking, catching, giving way, focal joint-line tenderness, symptoms worsening session over session.
- **Load principle:** ACWR 0.8-1.3 across total weekly load. Two matches plus two strength sessions plus one easy jog is the ceiling. Do not add high-intensity cardio.

## 8. Nutrition Anchors

- Protein floor: 115 g/day
- Collagen: 10-15 g taken 30-60 min before training or matches, paired with ~50 mg vitamin C. Counts toward daily protein total.

## App Data Notes (for developer handoff)

- Session types: `match`, `strength_moderate`, `strength_primary`, `recovery`, `jog`, `mobility_block`, `post_match_mobility`
- `mobility_block` occurs daily and should be a persistent daily checklist independent of the primary session.
- Strength exercises need per-week load values (see Section 2 progression table) with a Tuesday multiplier of ~0.85 rounded to 5 lb increments.
- Advancement rules map to two boolean check-ins per strength session: `form_clean` (knee tracking) and a next-morning `symptom_check`. A failed symptom check locks load progression for one week.
- Monthly recurring task: WBLT spot check with left/right numeric entry and computed asymmetry; trigger alert if asymmetry > 0.5 in.
- Timer requirements: interval timers for stretch holds (30/45 sec), tempo cues optional, 60 sec section rest timers, 90 sec set rest timers.
