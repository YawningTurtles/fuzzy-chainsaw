# Hip-to-Knee Program: Revised Routine (September 2026)

Supersedes the April 2026 assessment protocol and the flow-based daily mobility block. Revision driven by the updated evidence review plus current status: right medial knee pain much reduced and non-limiting in matches, ankle asymmetry closed to ~0.25 in, hip tightness and restricted kicking range now the primary complaint.

## What changed and why

| Change | Reason |
|---|---|
| Dropped MTP/toe mobilization | Mechanistic hypothesis only. No symptoms driving it. |
| Dropped foam rolling and massage gun as programmed work | Self-myofascial release produces acute, short-lived ROM change with no durable structural adaptation. Optional 60 to 90 sec primer, not counted as work. |
| Dropped gastroc and soleus wall stretches | Ankle asymmetry now ~0.25 in, inside measurement error. No gap left to close. |
| Ankle demoted from priority 1 to maintenance | Same reason. Banded talar-glide rocks retained on the left only. |
| Hips promoted to priority 1 | Current limiting complaint: kick height, stretching for a ball, hip/groin/hamstring/quad tightness. |
| Passive stretching replaced with loaded end-range work | Durable ROM comes from eccentric and loaded end-range loading, which increases ROM comparably to static stretching while strengthening tissue at length. |
| FIFA 11+ added as the actual pre-match warm-up, both matches | Highest-yield single intervention available. Meta-analysis of meta-analyses: 29% reduction in lower-limb injuries (RR 0.71), 46% reduction in knee injuries in the knee-specific meta-analysis. Effect is adherence-dependent at 2x/week, which two matches supplies. |
| Nordic and Copenhagen added | Nordic roughly halves hamstring injuries (RR 0.49). Copenhagen reliably builds eccentric hip adduction strength. Both are compliance-driven, not volume-driven, so low doses are correct. |
| Strength split to one focus per day, no rest days | At equal weekly volume, frequency is not a major independent driver of strength or hypertrophy. Splitting costs nothing and improves adherence. |

**Honest caveat carried forward:** a short daily block maintains strength well but under-doses genuine maximal-strength and hypertrophy adaptation for large muscles, which is volume-driven. If a real strength goal emerges, some sessions need to be consolidated and lengthened.

## Priority stack

1. Hip rotation and adductor end-range mobility and strength (daily)
2. Hip flexor length under load (daily)
3. FIFA 11+ at both matches
4. Posterior chain and unilateral quad strength (rotating, 3x/week across the split)
5. Left ankle dorsiflexion maintenance (daily, 2 min)

## Match schedule

- **Sunday:** 11-a-side, 50 minutes. Treated as the highest-load session of the week.
- **Wednesday:** 7-a-side, 40 minutes. Second-highest.

## Daily mobility block (~12 min, every day)

Hip-weighted. Loaded and active throughout. No passive holds longer than 30 seconds.

| Exercise | Dose | Cue |
|---|---|---|
| Left banded talar-glide knee-to-wall rocks | 2 x 15 | Drive the knee past the toes, heel stays down. Band pulls the talus back. |
| 90/90 active transitions, no hands | 8 slow per side | Rotate between sides without touching the floor. This is the strength half of hip rotation. |
| 90/90 ER hinge hold, front leg | 2 x 30 sec per side | Tall spine, hinge forward over the front shin. |
| Cossack squat or adductor rock-back | 8 per side | Sink into the bottom and own it. Do not bounce. |
| Half-kneeling hip flexor with PPT and overhead reach | 2 x 30 sec per side | Squeeze the rear glute first, tuck the tailbone, then reach overhead. Front of the rear hip, not the low back. |
| Hip CARs | 3 per direction per side | Largest possible arc, 10 to 15 sec per circle. No pelvic compensation. Doubles as a daily self-check. |

Optional: 60 to 90 sec of foam roller or massage gun before the block as a primer. Not a substitute for any element above.

## Weekly split

One focus per day. Roughly 10 to 15 minutes of strength, plus the mobility block.

### Sunday: match (11-a-side)
FIFA 11+ as the warm-up. No separate strength work.

### Monday: hip rotation and adductor end-range
24 hours post-match. Deliberately low fatigue.

| Exercise | Sets x reps | Tempo | Cues | Equipment | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cossack squat | 3 x 8/side | 3-1-2 | Heel down on the working side, sink to depth, control the return. | Bodyweight, progress to 15 lb goblet | 45 sec |
| Loaded 90/90 transition | 3 x 6/side | Slow | No hands. Drive from the hip, not momentum. | Mat | 45 sec |
| Adductor squeeze isometric | 5 x 20 sec | Isometric | Ball or rolled towel between the knees, 70% effort. | Mat | 30 sec |

### Tuesday: core anti-extension and upper
No lower-body loading the day before Wednesday.

| Exercise | Sets x reps | Tempo | Cues | Equipment | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dead bug with band | 3 x 8/side | 2-0-2 | Low back stays flat on the mat throughout. Stop where it lifts. | Band, mat | 45 sec |
| Plank with PPT | 3 x 40 sec | Isometric | Actively tuck the tailbone. If it is easy, it is not being cued. | Mat | 45 sec |
| DB pullover | 3 x 10 | 3-1-2 | Stop the moment the low back wants to arch off the bench. | Bench, 25 lb DB | 60 sec |
| Pull-ups or ring rows | 3 x max-2 | Controlled | Leave 2 in reserve. | Pull-up bar | 90 sec |

### Wednesday: match (7-a-side)
FIFA 11+ as the warm-up. No separate strength work.

### Thursday: eccentrics
72 hours before Sunday. This is the only slot that respects the no-novel-eccentrics-within-48h rule.

| Exercise | Sets x reps | Tempo | Cues | Equipment | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nordic hamstring | 2 x 5 | 4 sec lower | Hook the feet, lean forward, resist as long as possible, catch with the hands. | Bench or partner anchor | 90 sec |
| Copenhagen adduction, short lever | 2 x 6/side | 2-1-2 | Knee on the bench to start. Body in a straight line. | Bench, mat | 90 sec |

Hold both at this dose for three weeks minimum before progressing. Volume is not the driver here, consistency is.

### Friday: posterior chain, heavy
48 hours before Sunday. Heaviest day, but no grinding.

| Exercise | Sets x reps | Tempo | Cues | Equipment | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| DB Romanian deadlift | 3 x 8 @ 25 lbs | 3-1-2 | Hips straight back, slight knee bend. Load in the hamstrings and glutes. | Dumbbells | 90 sec |
| Single-leg calf raise, weighted | 3 x 12/side | 2-0-3 | Off a stair edge for full range at the bottom. | Stair, DB | 60 sec |

Leave 2 reps in reserve on every set. This is a hinge two days before a match, not a max-effort day.

### Saturday: unilateral quad and balance
Moderate. 24 hours before the 11-a-side.

| Exercise | Sets x reps | Tempo | Cues | Equipment | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bulgarian split squat | 3 x 8/side | 3-1-2 | Rear foot on the bench, front shin near vertical. Knee tracks over the second toe. | Bench, DBs | 75 sec |
| Spanish squat isometric | 3 x 30 sec | Isometric | Band behind the knees, anchored. Shins vertical, sit back into it. | Heavy band | 60 sec |
| Single-leg balance, eyes closed | 3 x 30 sec/side | Isometric | Slight knee bend. This is 11+ transfer work. | None | 45 sec |

Run at both matches. Audio-cued version in the companion HTML file, no phone-watching required.

2. Hip out, 45 sec
3. Hip in, 45 sec
4. Sideways shuffle, 45 sec, switch direction halfway
5. Forward and backward sprints, 45 sec

**Part 2, strength, plyometrics, balance (6:00)**
6. Nordic hamstring, 3 reps, 60 sec
7. Single-leg balance, 30 sec per side
8. Single-leg squat, 5 per side, 90 sec
9. Side plank, 30 sec per side
10. Vertical jumps, soft landings, 45 sec
11. Lateral jumps over a line, 45 sec

**Part 3, running (3:30)**
12. Run across the pitch at 75%, 60 sec
13. Bounding, 45 sec
14. Plant and cut, 60 sec
15. Sharp accelerations, 45 sec

Cut from the full program: shoulder-contact and partner drills (no partner), the duplicated running laps, and the plank and hip-lift stack. Retained: Nordic, balance, landing mechanics, and cutting, which carry the independent evidence.

## Progression rules

All criteria-based, never calendar-based.

- **Strength load:** complete all prescribed reps with ~2 in reserve on the top set for two consecutive sessions, then add the next 5 lb increment.
- **Copenhagen:** short lever (knee on bench) to long lever (foot on bench) when 2 x 6 is clean and pain-free, minimum three weeks at the current dose.
- **Nordic:** reduce hand assistance or increase range when the full eccentric is controlled for 5 reps.
- **Cossack:** bodyweight to 15 lb goblet when full depth is held for 3 sec without heel lift.
- **Hip rotation:** progress the 90/90 transition to a 5 lb ankle load or a slow tempo increase when 3 x 6 per side is smooth without hands.
- **Standing rule:** never add novel high-volume eccentrics within 48 hours of a match.

## Pain rules, right medial knee

- **During exercise:** 3/10 or less, steady or fading, is acceptable. Continue.
- **Above 3/10, or sharp, catching, or rising through a set:** stop that exercise. Substitute an isometric (Spanish squat, wall sit) or a pain-free range.
- **24 hours after:** back to baseline within 24 hours means the load was tolerated. Worse than baseline beyond 24 hours means cut that exercise's volume by half next session.
- **Escalation:** two consecutive weeks of no improvement, or worsening despite load management, is a red flag.
- **Stop and see a clinician:** locking, giving way, effusion or swelling, night pain or rest pain, inability to bear weight, pain reaching and staying above 5/10, or a discrete traumatic pop with rapid swelling.

## Running

Match sprinting is a harder stimulus than jogging, and it is currently symptom-free. Return-to-running gates are met in substance, so Phase 1 of the walk-to-run protocol is skipped.

- Start at 4 min run / 1 min walk x 6, three sessions per week maximum
- Metronome at 5 to 10% above current cadence
- 24-hour symptom check after every session before advancing
- Cap weekly volume growth at 10%
- Never run the day before Sunday's match during the first four weeks

## Reassessment, 6 and 12 weeks

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Hip rotation, prone, inclinometer app | Numeric baseline first. Then 85 deg or more total per hip, under 8 deg asymmetry. |
| Kicking range, subjective | Higher kick, less restriction reaching for a ball. Note it after matches. |
| Cossack squat depth | Full depth held 3 sec, heel down, both sides |
| Left ankle knee-to-wall | Hold within 0.5 in of the right |
| Single-leg hop for distance, L vs R | Better validated asymmetry metric than the wall sit. Establish a baseline. |
| Right knee pain, 0 to 10, during and 24h after a match | Trending down, longer pain-free stretches |
| Adductor squeeze test | Establish a baseline, track against Copenhagen progression |

Also worth adding: a landing quality check (single-leg drop, watch for the knee caving inward), since the 11+ trains neuromuscular control rather than strength alone.

## Standing caveats

- No diagnosis exists for the right medial knee. The pain rules are the safety net, not a treatment plan.
- The injury-prevention evidence (11+, Nordic, Copenhagen) comes overwhelmingly from youth and young-adult male soccer cohorts. Mechanisms generalize to a 39-year-old recreational player; the specific effect sizes were not measured in that demographic.
- Hip strengthening is justified by the pain literature, not by a prevention claim. Correcting hip weakness has never been shown to prevent knee injury.
- The asymmetry-to-injury link is weak. Closing gaps is defensible on its own merits, not as risk reduction.
