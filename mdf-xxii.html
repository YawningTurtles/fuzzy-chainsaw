How I scored the Paige scale

1 to 10, guessing from Dimmu Borgir, In Flames, Dark Tranquillity, Amon Amarth, Children of Bodom, HammerFall, Arch Enemy, Blind Guardian, plus Wolfheart, Orbit Culture, One of Nine, Night of the Vampire and Witch Club Satan — and against metalcore, whiny vocals, and heavy-with-no-ideas.

9–10 melody, atmosphere and theatre; the reason to buy the ticket. 7–8 strong odds, might become a regular. 5–6 worth standing through once, historically interesting. 3–4 respect it, won't play it at home. 1–2 a wall of noise with no tune in it.

The eighteen names I originally came up empty on are now researched and marked with a red tag. Three of my first guesses were flat wrong: Crud is sludge and death-doom rather than grind, Lay Waste is deathgrind rather than brutal death, and Hong Kong Fuck You is a Tijuana powerviolence band with three bassists and no guitarist. Blood Vial and Bogside Sniper Squadron are still thinly sourced and worth re-checking nearer the date. Angels Rock Bar and pre-fest bands are still to be announced, so this will grow.
