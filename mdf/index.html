How the Paige scale works

Every band starts at 5. Melodic twin-guitar leads, keyboards and symphonic texture, cold Scandinavian atmosphere, epic scope and real hooks push it up. Second-wave Scandinavian lineage — Mayhem, Arcturus, Emperor, Dimmu Borgir, Dissection, Windir — pushes it up hardest of all, and cancels the penalty for lo-fi production.

Traditional thrash, technical and progressive death metal, pure brutality, dissonance and lo-fi as an end in itself pull it down. Emo, whiny or metalcore vibes are a hard veto.
